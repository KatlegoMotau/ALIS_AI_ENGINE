# ALIS – Artificial Legal Intelligence System

> An AI-powered legal document analysis platform that helps users identify compliance issues, improve legal documents, and generate intelligent compliance reports.

---

## Overview

ALIS (Artificial Legal Intelligence System) is a full-stack web application developed to assist individuals and organisations in analysing legal and policy documents using Artificial Intelligence.

The system automatically reviews uploaded documents against predefined legal requirements, identifies potential compliance issues, and generates detailed reports with recommendations to help users improve their documents.

This project was developed as part of my final-year Information Technology studies and demonstrates the integration of modern web technologies with AI to solve a real-world problem.

---

## Problem Statement

Reviewing legal documents manually is time-consuming and requires specialised legal knowledge. Small businesses, students, and individuals often struggle to determine whether their documents comply with relevant legal standards.

ALIS addresses this challenge by providing an intelligent document analysis platform capable of identifying potential issues and suggesting areas for improvement.

## How It Works

1. User uploads a legal document.
2. Spring Boot processes the request.
3. The document is sent to the FastAPI AI Engine.
4. The AI analyses the content against legal rules and requirements.
5. A compliance report is generated.
6. Results are stored in PostgreSQL and displayed to the user.
